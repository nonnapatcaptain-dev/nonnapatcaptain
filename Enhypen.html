About the Group

ENHYPEN (엔하이픈) is a 7-member South Korean boy group under BELIFT LAB formed through the survival reality competition show I-LAND in 2020.

The name represents connection, discovery, and growth—like a hyphen connecting words to create new meanings.

Debut Date: November 30, 2020 • Official Fandom: ENGENE
